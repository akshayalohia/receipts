# Reply writing

- One reply per lead. Under 200 characters for X.
- Name the actual win in their words ("10k subs", "#1 on Product Hunt"), then say we already made the trophy, then the link.
- Example: "Congrats on 10k! We turned this post into a trophy already, have a look: <claim_url>"
- No hashtags, no "check out our store", no fake urgency, no pretending to be a fan account.
- Use only numbers that appear in the lead data.
- Emails: 3-4 sentences, subject "We made you a trophy for <win>", end with "Reply 'no' and we won't write again."
- Team angle for company news: mention the $249 team pack once, after the personal trophy.
